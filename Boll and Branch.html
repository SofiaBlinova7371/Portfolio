Mosaic Waffle Comforter launch Campaign

Campaign proposal for the launch of Boll & Branch’s Waffle Mosaic Comforter, developed across organic and paid social media, static and motion assets, and email marketing.

The concept focuses on translating the product’s tactile qualities, breathable cotton construction, and elevated aesthetic into a cohesive digital campaign designed to build awareness, drive engagement, and support product launch across multiple customer touchpoints.
